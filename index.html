🕐 Working hours & schedule settings
Only the hours you actually trade are shown on the Weekly Schedule grid — the rest (e.g. 11PM–7AM) is collapsed, and days off are hidden entirely, so the whole week fits on one screen without much scrolling, especially on mobile. Rows automatically stretch to fill the remaining space when your hours are short.

Days off
Mark the days you don't trade so they're grayed out on the calendar — they stay visible (it's still a calendar), just visually out of the way. Choose Always for a recurring weekday (applies from today onward, not retroactively to past dates), or One day only for a single date, like a public holiday.
